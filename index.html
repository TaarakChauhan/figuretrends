Nendoroid Hinako Shimizu (Silent Hill f)

Good Smile Nendoroid #3097. Official shop bonus window was set to close mid September 2026. Small, cheap entry if you play the new Silent Hill and want one piece on the shelf.

Still circulating from summer ranks

Hololive Omaru Polka 1/7

Showed up in AmiAmi mid year figure ranking. VTuber scales move fast when a talent is touring or clipping well. Aftermarket usually jumps if the first run is thin.

Safe popular IP

Marin Kitagawa (My Dress Up Darling)

New prize and Nendoroid variants are still coming through 2026. Prize figures are cheap display pieces. Scales and Nendoroids hold value better.

Verdict: Prize version equals fun, low risk. Scale only if this is a character you display long term.

Check Amazon.ca for Marin Kitagawa figures

WonFes leftover heat

Re:Zero Emilia Wedding Dress 1/7 (Design COCO)

Revealed at Wonder Festival 2026 Summer. Wedding versions of main heroines usually preorder well and do not sit on shelves.

Verdict: Watch the preorder open date. These sell on announcement, not on release.

Check Amazon.ca for Emilia figures

Game crossover

NIKKE scales (Anise / Nayuta and other 2026 lines)

NIKKE keeps getting large Kotobukiya and event scales. Collectors buy the character they main. Random NIKKE scales are easier to miss if you do not play.

Verdict: Buy your main. Skip “it looks cool” unless you have shelf space and budget.

Check Amazon.ca for NIKKE figures

Budget / body kit

Romankey UMS articulated female body 1/12

Hit AmiAmi reservation ranks because customizers use these under kits and clothes. Not a character figure. Different buyer.

Verdict: Buy only if you customize. Most anime collectors can ignore this.

Check Amazon.ca for 1/12 figure bodies

Wait

Generic original character bunny or bath scales

These rank on raw preorder volume and then flood the used market. Pretty sculpt, weak resale unless the artist is a name you already collect.

Verdict: Wait 6 months after release and look at used prices before you spend ¥20,000+.

Canada landed cost note Prices on Figure Trends are shop ranges in yen / rough USD. They are not your final Canada cost. For Japan to Canada by mail, CBSA generally starts assessing tax above CAD $20. You usually owe GST or HST (and PST in some provinces). Duty on Japanese origin toys and figures is often low or zero under CPTPP when origin is declared correctly, not guaranteed. Canada Post often adds about CAD $9.95 if it collects tax. Couriers can add higher brokerage. Always recheck the shop, shipping method, and CBSA rules before you buy. This is not tax or legal advice.
