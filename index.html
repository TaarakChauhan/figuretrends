Canada landed cost checklist (tap to expand)

Use this before you click buy on a Japan shop. Plain language only. Not tax or legal advice. Rules and fees change. Recheck CBSA and your courier before you pay.

  1. Shop price in yen. Copy the tax in yen price from AmiAmi or the maker. Convert to CAD with a rate you trust today. That is only the first line.
  2. International shipping. Add EMS, DHL, or whatever method the shop offers to Canada. Bigger boxes cost more. Surface mail is slower and still not free.
  3. GST or HST, and PST where it applies. For mail into Canada, tax is commonly assessed when the shipment is over about CAD $20. Your province rate matters. This is not a full tax guide.
  4. Brokerage / collection fee. If Canada Post collects tax, many people see about CAD $9.95 on top. Couriers can charge higher brokerage. Build that line in before you celebrate a yen discount.
  5. Duty. CBSA often looks at imports above that low mail threshold. Duty on Japan origin toys and figures is often low or zero under CPTPP when origin is declared correctly. Often is not guaranteed. Confirm for your shipment.
  6. Write the sum before you click. Figure + shipping + tax + brokerage (+ duty if any) = landed cost. If that number hurts, Wait. Do not invent a cheaper story in your head at checkout.

Prices on Figure Trends are shop ranges or heat notes in yen context. They are not your final Canada cost.
