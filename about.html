Who runs this

Figure Trends is an independent list run by Taarak. It is a one person project. There is no company storefront behind it.

What Figure Trends is

A weekly Buy / Skip / Wait shortlist for anime and game collectible figures. The goal is to help collectors decide what to preorder before windows close, with Canada shipping and tax reality in mind.

How Buy / Skip / Wait is chosen

Each week we look at reservation heat (especially AmiAmi monthly and related ranking noise), official shop windows, and whether a piece is actually worth the money for a normal collector. Editorial judgment matters. Rank alone is not enough. We call Canada landed cost when it changes the decision.

What this is not

Not a shop. Not financial advice. Not an official partner of Good Smile, AmiAmi, Kotobukiya, or any manufacturer. Some Amazon.ca links are affiliate links and may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
